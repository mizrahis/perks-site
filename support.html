Perks support

Perks is free and has no adverts. It helps you keep track of the benefits you already pay for.

Contact us

Please say which version of Perks you use and what you expected to happen. Please do not send passwords or full card numbers.

Common questions

  • Reminders. Allow notifications for Perks in your phone’s Settings. You choose the hour and quiet hours on You.
  • Gmail. Connect or disconnect on You. You can also remove Perks’ access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • AI processing. You can review or withdraw permission for what Perks sends to its AI services on You, under AI processing.
  • Delete your account. Open You and choose Delete account. Your account and what Perks holds about you are deleted now; uploaded images are removed by our server within a day; if a removal fails we keep trying and are alerted.
